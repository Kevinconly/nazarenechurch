# Official brand assets

The supplied original PNG artwork is stored in `public/brand/` without edits.

- `nazarene-logo-grey.png` — official grey mark for light backgrounds.
- `nazarene-logo-extended-grey.png` — extended grey mark for light backgrounds and footer use.
- `nazarene-logo-white.png` — white mark for dark backgrounds.
- `nazarene-logo-extended-white.png` — extended white mark for dark backgrounds.
- `nazarene-logo-black.png` — supplied black mark; use only where a monochrome black mark is appropriate.

Use the `Logo` component to select variants. No Remera church-name logo or licensed Brandon Grotesque font was supplied. Do not recreate either; identify the local church in surrounding page text and use Lato as the heading fallback.
